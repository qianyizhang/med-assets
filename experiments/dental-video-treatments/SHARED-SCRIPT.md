# Before the hole

## Narration script and shot plan

48-second educational prototype. Same spoken script and audio are used for the creatively different HyperFrames, Remotion and ManimGL treatments.

### 00–06 seconds: The hook

**Voice:** Tooth decay starts before a hole. What tips the balance?

**HyperFrames treatment:** Dimensional hero molar, orbiting sugar and bacteria; camera pushes toward enamel.

### 06–14 seconds: The acid factory

**Voice:** Some bacteria in plaque use sugars to make acids. Repeated exposure can keep enamel losing minerals.

**HyperFrames treatment:** Macro enamel terrain; sugar cubes arrive; some plaque bacteria produce moving acid particles; mineral bricks separate.

### 14–23 seconds: The tug-of-war

**Voice:** Saliva and fluoride help replace lost minerals. Early decay can sometimes be stopped or reversed before a cavity forms.

**HyperFrames treatment:** Split-screen loss and repair; enamel mineral tile mosaic; saliva droplets and fluoride shield counter orange acid particles.

### 23–31 seconds: The threshold

**Voice:** If loss keeps winning, the surface can break down. A formed cavity needs dental assessment and appropriate treatment.

**HyperFrames treatment:** A macro tooth cross-section reveals the surface-breakdown threshold, with a bounded cavity and direct enamel, dentin and pulp labels.

### 31–40 seconds: The clinical decision

**Voice:** A dentist assesses the tooth and selects treatment. A filling is one common way to restore damaged structure.

**HyperFrames treatment:** Mirror enters; three assessment cards; conditional restoration sequence; pulp remains intact and distinct.

### 40–48 seconds: The long game

**Voice:** The long game is prevention: fluoride toothpaste twice daily, cleaning between teeth, and regular dental review.

**HyperFrames treatment:** Brush sweep; fluoride toothpaste, floss and calendar props orbit restored tooth; concluding prevention checklist.

## Audio

Synthetic, non-cloned Kokoro af_heart voice generated locally. Original procedural music and transition accents, mixed beneath narration. No paid service or commercial music samples. This is a narrated prototype, not a live clinician recording. Timing and loudness were checked; automated tools here could not listen to the final performance.

## Clinical boundaries

The story distinguishes early mineral loss from a formed cavity. It shows one possible restorative pathway after assessment, not a universal treatment sequence. It does not teach operative technique or claim validated anatomy, exact biochemical kinetics, or a guaranteed result. A qualified dental reviewer should approve it before patient-facing publication.
