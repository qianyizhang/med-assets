# Ultrasound anatomy tours

Building or changing a sweep or tour starts with the [3D modeling standard](../../docs/gallery/use-cases/ultrasound-anatomy.md#3d-modeling-standard-for-sweeps-and-tours). This README owns the local replay commands and review workflow.

Open the [local tour](http://127.0.0.1:8874/index.html) for abdomen, thyroid, carotid, breast and pregnancy. The [bilateral carotid duplex exam](http://127.0.0.1:8874/carotid-survey/) adds gated velocity measurements and a reconstructed volume from recorded transverse sections. The [pregnancy sequence](http://127.0.0.1:8874/index.html?sweep=pregnancy-early) includes eleven sweeps across four gestational windows. The stage links open the early pregnancy, first-trimester anatomy, mid-trimester survey and third-trimester sequences. The probe, patient and imaging field occupy one 3D scene, with simulated and acquired ultrasound images below. The camera orbits the selected organ or the named region of a larger vessel. **Oblique**, **Axial**, **Coronal** and **Sagittal** set the 3D viewing direction. Select a preset again after orbiting to restore its framing. Changing the camera preserves the selected ultrasound frame and torso opacity.

Choose [Tours](http://127.0.0.1:8874/tours/index.html) to play an entire visit, scrub its acquisitions and return from an impression to the measured frame. [Worked cases](http://127.0.0.1:8874/cases/index.html) contains companion plates and acquired examples. [Case packages and verification](cases/README.md)

Scrub the timeline, play the sweep, or jump directly to a named reference tick. **Anatomy overlay** identifies the tissue intersected by that plane, using CT labels, atlas surfaces or the pregnancy model’s tissue boundaries. Each acquired image retains its own subject, scale and orientation. Differences in plane, level and crop are identified on the card or in Sources. **Keyframe assets** groups the current frame into 3D anatomy, simulated scan and acquired ultrasound. Open **Related anatomy** on a supported sweep for additional acquired views and their credits. Returning to the tour preserves the selected frame. This inspector supplies the detail behind the collection-wide [Motion and B-mode rendering reviews](../../docs/gallery/use-cases/ultrasound-anatomy.md#development-review). [Full tour specification](../../docs/gallery/use-cases/ultrasound-anatomy.md) · [Reference records](research/README.md)

## Teaching and Development

Open **Three landmarks in the liver window** to follow one continuous medial sweep. Compare the current B-mode frame with the pinned opening plane. Select a landmark to check its identity and open long- or short-axis evidence from its dedicated sweep. Play across sections, scrub the whole tour, or select a named section mark. **Open standalone sweep** opens the current source frame. In [Bilateral thyroid orientation](http://127.0.0.1:8874/tours/thyroid-orientation/index.html), each lobe sweep supplies two consecutive sections: slide from the isthmus, then rotate into long axis. [Tours → AAA screening](http://127.0.0.1:8874/tours/aaa/index.html) uses the same tour controls for three cases. Play the negative screen, follow the positive case through diagnostic views, or replay the limited window and repeat attempt in the indeterminate case. In the complete build, all three cases use the normal aortic sweep’s fitted torso, curved probe and sector. The same pose determines the 3D field and the simulated B-mode frame. A source CT supplies surrounding tissue after the native vessels are removed locally. The authored vessels supply case-specific wall, lumen, thrombus and calipers. The report links open the exact vascular sweep and frame. All [exam families](cases/README.md) open from Tours. Each case has one timeline from referral through acquisitions to the impression. Use the acquisition picker on longer visits to jump directly to a named view. Each saved finding opens its source frame. Existing `exams/` bookmarks forward to the corresponding tour.

Open **Development reviews** to inspect the sweep collection. [Asset qualification](http://127.0.0.1:8874/development/assets.html) groups reusable anatomical assets by body region and the selected tours or standalone sweeps. Open an asset to compare its parts and fitted variants with their reviewed baselines and sources. **Motion** plays a short, fixed-camera clip for each sweep with its probe, imaging field and B-mode together. **B-mode rendering** groups named frames by sweep and pairs the full simulated image with its acquired comparison. Filter by region or select a sweep to inspect larger pairs. Open a keyframe to see its exact pose. The separate cardiac and duplex exam players have their own acquisition and review packages.

**Study coverage** opens the full exam chapter index. **Browse geometry bindings** groups repeated use of a part and shape, with source revisions and links to the individual acquisitions. The [reconciliation policy](../../docs/gallery/use-cases/ultrasound-anatomy.md#development-review) defines all five provenance labels. The [illustration set](development/illustrations/README.md) records the ImageGen prompts and display scope.

### Qualify a 3D part

Open **Development → Asset qualification**. Choose a body region and select tours or standalone sweeps from the grouped filter. Click an asset on the shelf to see its parts together in 3D. Toggle individual parts in **Asset**, or switch to **Scene** to toggle whole assets in the same acquisition. For an abdominal tour, inspect the referenced anatomy in its source frame. Choose **Availability → Rebuilds and missing parts** to see what each chapter still needs. The mobile-stone case, for example, reuses the s1233 gallbladder outline and lists the wall, lumen and mobile calculus separately. The acquisition picker changes the case or chapter. Part variants and assessments lead to the exact geometry, teaching use and review. [Qualification criteria](../../docs/gallery/use-cases/ultrasound-anatomy.md#development-review)

Assembly writes `development/asset-inventory.json` from the sweep and case records. The [abdominal chapter requirements](development/assets-abdomen.js) and [other exam chapter requirements](development/assets-exams.js) map each chapter to its original sweep, named Gallery surfaces, retained worked geometry and gaps. The [Gallery source packager](gallery_reference_sources.py) reads named surfaces from retained GLB editions into the workbench mesh format. Review packages in `development/asset-reviews/` retain the named task, baseline, reviewed revision, observations, rubric, reviewer and matched images. Keep one assessment for exactly matching part geometry and task evidence. List repeated case and chapter bindings in `additionalUses`. Seal the selected image bytes with their SHA-256 digests. A review of a modeled case binds to its assembled family recipe. An original sweep review binds to the delivered mesh digest and rubric. When those identities change, the previous evidence remains inspectable and the new revision starts unrated. The [workbench rules](../../docs/gallery/use-cases/ultrasound-anatomy.md#development-review) and [rubric](../../docs/guides/asset-quality.md) describe the judgments.

### Review a renderer change

1. Assemble and serve the baseline study using [Replay](#replay). Freeze its current comparison frames with the [batch helper](development/compare_renders.py). Pass `--region Abdomen` for a regional batch or omit the selection to capture every catalogued keyframe.
2. Use the owning sweep builder to regenerate the affected scans and assemble the candidate in a fresh directory. Compare it with the saved baseline. The helper checks the acquisition pose, field, labeled section, render dimensions and acquired reference before publishing a batch to the candidate's Development page.
3. Open **B-mode rendering → Renders → Before and after**. Inspect the previous and candidate render beside the same acquired frame, then open the sweep if the difference might be due to placement. Record which frames improved, regressed or remain uncertain, with the visible change in texture, boundary contrast or depth-dependent brightness. Retain decisive evidence under the [repository retention policy](../../docs/WORKFLOW.md#retention).

```sh
python experiments/ultrasound-sweep/development/compare_renders.py snapshot "$BASELINE_STUDY" "$BASELINE_FRAMES"
python experiments/ultrasound-sweep/development/compare_renders.py compare "$BASELINE_FRAMES" "$CANDIDATE_STUDY" \
  --label-before "Previous render" --label-after "Candidate render"
```

Generate Motion clips for a served build with [the capture script](capture_motion.mjs). It uses Playwright, Chrome and ffmpeg. Give it the served entry URL and that same build's clip directory. Append sweep IDs to refresh only those clips. Each five-second clip samples the sweep from first to last pose. Regenerate affected clips after a pose or renderer change. The video and poster files are local review outputs.

```sh
node experiments/ultrasound-sweep/capture_motion.mjs "$STUDY_URL" "$CANDIDATE_STUDY/development/clips"
node experiments/ultrasound-sweep/verify_development.mjs "$STUDY_URL"
node experiments/ultrasound-sweep/composed-tour/verify-tour.mjs "$STUDY_URL"
```

## Replay

For the governed liver surface, install the repository package into the sweep's Python environment so the builder and assembler can read the AssetKit record. Hydrate `models/liver_s1233/exports/r01/liver.mesh` with Git LFS. The [exact rebuild](../../models/liver_s1233/README.md#rebuild-and-verify) can recover a missing export from the source mask. The builder checks the package's export digest and voxel grid before placing it in the scene. The acquisition still samples the pinned CT and masks.

Use Python 3.12 with NumPy 2.2.6, SciPy 1.16.1, scikit-image 0.25.2 and Pillow 11.3.0. Retrieve the public TotalSegmentator v2.0.1 cases s1233 (abdomen) and s1127 (thyroid and common carotid), the two original gallbladder images linked in the reference study, and the hydrated LFS inputs for the atlas meshes and the other [acquired comparisons](research/acquired/). For breast, hydrate the [mammary atlas](../../models/breasts_hra/exports/teaching-economy-r01/README.md) and download the [female skin GLB](https://cdn.humanatlas.io/digital-objects/ref-organ/skin-female/v1.3/assets/3d-vh-f-skin.glb). Set `HRA_SKIN` to the downloaded file. The builder verifies the identities of both models. Run from the repository root with a fresh output directory.

```sh
python experiments/ultrasound-sweep/build_study.py "$S1233_CASE" "$LONG_AXIS_PNG" "$SHORT_AXIS_GIF" "$STUDY_OUTPUT"
python experiments/ultrasound-sweep/prepare_scenes.py "$STUDY_OUTPUT" --vendor-dir lessons/one-breath/.local/vendor
python experiments/ultrasound-sweep/build_abdomen.py "$S1233_CASE" "$STUDY_OUTPUT/sweeps"
python experiments/ultrasound-sweep/build_aortic_screening.py "$S1233_CASE" "$STUDY_OUTPUT/sweeps"
python experiments/ultrasound-sweep/aaa_acquisition.py "$S1233_CASE" "$STUDY_OUTPUT" "$STUDY_OUTPUT/aaa-acquisitions"
python experiments/ultrasound-sweep/build_thyroid.py "$S1127_CASE" "$STUDY_OUTPUT/sweeps"
python experiments/ultrasound-sweep/build_carotid.py "$S1127_CASE" "$STUDY_OUTPUT/sweeps"
python experiments/ultrasound-sweep/build_breast.py "$STUDY_OUTPUT/sweeps" --skin "$HRA_SKIN"
python experiments/ultrasound-sweep/build_axilla.py "$STUDY_OUTPUT/sweeps" --skin "$HRA_SKIN"
python experiments/ultrasound-sweep/build_early_pregnancy.py "$STUDY_OUTPUT/sweeps"
python experiments/ultrasound-sweep/build_pregnancy.py "$STUDY_OUTPUT/sweeps"
python experiments/ultrasound-sweep/build_gestational.py "$STUDY_OUTPUT/sweeps"
# Optional: generate the bilateral carotid duplex acquisition here before assembly.
python experiments/ultrasound-sweep/assemble_tours.py "$STUDY_OUTPUT"
python -m http.server 8874 --bind 127.0.0.1 --directory "$STUDY_OUTPUT"
```

The [AAA acquisition recipe](aaa_acquisition.py) needs the same pinned s1233 source as the normal aortic builder. Use a fresh bundle directory. Assembly picks up `$STUDY_OUTPUT/aaa-acquisitions` or accepts another verified copy with `--aaa-acquisitions PATH`. The bundle binds the case recipe, native meshes, per-chapter poses and render files. A smaller build that omits it retains modeled B-mode and identifies the unavailable shared-torso view. The complete build keeps B-mode playable if the 3D view cannot load.

For the original nine abdominal sweeps, omit the aortic screening and other regional builders. The two thyroid lobes, the two carotid sides, the two breast routes and each pregnancy builder’s routes are assembled as complete sets. For the combined carotid exam, build the [registered atlas acquisition](carotid-survey/README.md#replay) in the same staging tree before assembly. Both assembly modes include cardiac echo and require its [hydrated LFS inputs](cardiac/README.md#local-replay).

For a compact serving directory, run `python experiments/ultrasound-sweep/assemble_tours.py "$STUDY_OUTPUT" --delivery "$TOUR_OUTPUT"` and serve `$TOUR_OUTPUT`. Choose a fresh destination. The compact build includes one copy of each byte-identical sweep mesh, every distinct torso fit, and the runtime images. Keep the generated receipts and source inputs with the staging tree for replay.

Open [the local study](http://127.0.0.1:8874/index.html). The scene preparation step checks the repository's pinned Three.js runtime and license. Omit `--vendor-dir` to retrieve those bytes if a staged copy is unavailable. WebGL 2 renders the spatial view. The scan timeline and image comparisons also work when it cannot load.

The abdomen builders sample 49 planes per sweep on the s1233 grid (1.5 mm per voxel, RAS axes). The contact is where the central ray meets the patient surface. The 3D sector and image sampler share its contact, direction and depth. The [track data](abdomen-tracks.json) identifies the target, context, section palette, camera focus, depth and named keyframes. The gallbladder retains its original paired render. Each additional sweep has a verification receipt alongside its generated frames. The projections and overlay share the structure palette. Rebuild a sweep after changing its pose or section colors. The catalogue assembler verifies the acquired derivatives against their hashes and checks the input inventory before writing its tour and reference indexes.

The [normal aortic survey](http://127.0.0.1:8874/index.html?sweep=aortic-screening) takes probe contacts from the s1233 CT and samples the same-case aortic and caval masks, iliac arteries and vertebrae. A rib-registered BodyParts3D torso conforms to those probe contacts. Its three transverse stops follow the artery along its local centerline. **AP span** projects the native segmented contour’s anterior–posterior extent onto the scan and its anatomy overlay. Run `python experiments/ultrasound-sweep/verify_aortic_screening.py "$S1233_CASE" "$STUDY_OUTPUT/sweeps/aortic-screening"` to verify physical calibration, contour intersections and probe contact. [Coverage and interpretation](../../docs/gallery/use-cases/ultrasound-anatomy.md#normal-aortic-screening-survey)

The [thyroid builder](build_thyroid.py) samples the [two lobe tracks](thyroid-tracks.json) on the s1127 RAS grid. Its linear probe samples a rectangle with equal horizontal and vertical pixel spacing. The [fitted head and shoulders](display_neck.py) use the BodyParts3D exterior registered to s1127 cervical, thoracic and rib landmarks. The contact patch follows the case skin. A rigid footprint’s center and edges meet the curved skin at different depths. Each receipt records that difference and the anatomy present at the reference stops. [Thyroid acquisitions](research/README.md#thyroid)

The [right](http://127.0.0.1:8874/index.html?sweep=carotid-right) and [left common carotid](http://127.0.0.1:8874/index.html?sweep=carotid-left) sweeps use the s1127 CT for probe placement, artery geometry and grayscale tissue context. Follow the linear probe from a transverse section into a longitudinal view. At a reference stop, **Magnify section** resamples a smaller physical field through the artery and outlines that region in 3D. The right jugular segment is traced from the same CT. Run `python experiments/ultrasound-sweep/verify_carotid.py "$S1127_CASE" "$STUDY_OUTPUT/sweeps"` to check the source trace, scan geometry and seated probe footprint. [Carotid sources and comparison limits](research/README.md#common-carotid-artery)

The [breast builder](build_breast.py) produces a four-pass layer survey and an independently steerable nipple and duct route. Each pose stores a two-dimensional height grid for the probe footprint in millimetres. This grid flattens the displayed skin and anatomy against the probe face. Inverting the same map through depth gives the tissue coordinates for the simulated scan and section overlay. The focused route samples a second set at 3 mm of additional probe displacement. Its HRA nipple has a modeled base enclosing the native anterior surface. The [axilla builder](build_axilla.py) uses the female exterior and an authored node, vessels and muscle. Its per-pose imaging depth changes from a shallow nodal portrait to a deeper regional section. Both the rectangular 3D field and raster sampling use that depth. The survey, nipple and axillary routes share the Breast picker group. [Tour controls and coverage](../../docs/gallery/use-cases/ultrasound-anatomy.md#breast-coverage) · [Breast sources](research/README.md#breast)

The pregnancy builders use independent maternal and fetal geometry at about 7, 12, 20 and 32 weeks. The earliest [track](early-pregnancy-track.json) follows the gestational sac, embryo and cardiac region. The [12-week tracks](pregnancy-tracks.json) extend the existing profile and placenta routes. The [mid- and late-trimester tracks](gestational-tracks.json) define the modeled lie, probe orientation, target and purpose of each stop. To reproduce the complete set, run all three pregnancy commands above into a staging tree with fresh pregnancy output directories and hydrate the acquired WebPs in Git LFS.

At a magnifiable stop, the builder samples the physical crop at the full image resolution. Each emitted scan stays registered to the original frame. The [early checks](verify_early_pregnancy.py) and [12-week checks](verify_pregnancy.py) accept `"$STUDY_OUTPUT/sweeps"` and compare exported triangle cuts against the image sampler. The [mid/late builder](build_gestational.py) records target and contact coordinates and crop bounds with each sweep. Run `python experiments/ultrasound-sweep/verify_gestational.py "$STUDY_OUTPUT/sweeps"` to check the delivered mid/late meshes and both image magnifications against the recorded poses. Serve a compact pregnancy build with `python -m http.server 8878 --bind 127.0.0.1 --directory "$TOUR_OUTPUT"`. [Learning objectives and direct routes](../../docs/gallery/use-cases/ultrasound-anatomy.md#pregnancy-coverage)

The abdominal and thyroid simulations use CT-derived tissue interfaces and illustrative brightness. Acoustic properties, pressure and respiration are unmeasured. The fitted [BodyParts3D exterior](../../models/skin/exports/bp3d-envelope-r01/README.md) is an illustrative body around the CT organs. The [gallbladder fit](display_body.py) uses ribs 6–10 and conforms its contact window to the CT surface. For the eight additional abdominal sweeps, the [contact-window fit](fit_abdomen.py) checks the first intersection of each incoming beam with the exterior. The lateral kidney and left posterior splenic windows use a staged arm position to expose the flank. Each sweep records its exterior and contact error. These are illustrative placements, with independent technique review still open. The earlier Blender pose plates remain historical evidence.

Run `python experiments/ultrasound-sweep/verify_assembly.py "$STUDY_OUTPUT"` against a fresh build to check the compact delivery, reference bindings and rejection of stale or corrupt inputs. For a build with all three breast routes, run `python experiments/ultrasound-sweep/verify_breast_contact.py "$STUDY_OUTPUT"` to ray-test the deformed delivery meshes. With the built study served locally, run `node experiments/ultrasound-sweep/verify_playback.mjs` in the Gallery Playwright environment. Set `MODEL_SURVEY_PLAYWRIGHT_MODULE` or `MODEL_SURVEY_BROWSER` to use an installed browser. Verification walks all sweep routes, reference ticks, overlays, camera presets, playback, asset return, responsive layout and unavailable-media states.
