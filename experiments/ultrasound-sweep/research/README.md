# Gallbladder sweep reference study

Use the acquired image for each reference keyframe to judge the shape of the lumen, brightness and definition of the wall, surrounding tissue, and depth. The acquisition lead-in and intermediate rotations are modeled transitions. The control names the closest endpoint when a reference stays on screen during a transition.

The [build recipe](../build_study.py) verifies the original image identities, CT, same-case labels, and grid. The extra [rib hashes](rib-pins.json) are from the s1233 source receipt. The CT and segmentation source is [TotalSegmentator v2.0.1](https://zenodo.org/records/10047263), Jakob Wasserthal, contributors and University Hospital Basel, CC BY 4.0. The earlier fixed-camera prototype used a small regional locator from the anterior view of the [reviewed BodyParts3D skin export](../../../models/skin/exports/teaching-economy-r01/README.md), © DBCLS, [CC BY 4.0](https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/en/bodyparts3d/lic.html). That historical inset indicated the region on a separate adult atlas. The live study uses the [full-resolution BodyParts3D exterior](../../../models/skin/exports/bp3d-envelope-r01/README.md) and [atlas skeleton](../../../models/skeleton/exports/teaching-economy-r01/README.md), under the same DBCLS terms. Right ribs 6–10 supply the atlas-to-case fit. The fitted contact window follows the CT skin. The internal organs, probe track and sector use the s1233 frame.

The different cases support visual comparisons. Specialist review of probe technique, ultrasound appearance and use for teaching is open. The experiment provides no disease examples or quantitative measurement task.
