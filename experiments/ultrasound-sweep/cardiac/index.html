Reading this view & sources

The left ventricle forms the apex on screen right. The right ventricle sits on screen left, with its tricuspid attachment closer to the apex. In the overlay, LV and RV mark ventricles, LA and RA mark atria.

Pause at end diastole and end systole. Compare the left ventricular cavity and the distance from apex to valve plane. At early filling, look for the mitral leaflets separating. The outflow valves lie outside this section.

The model and B-mode texture are illustrative. Phase links align visible landmarks across cases. Rotation changes only the modeled plane. Flow timing describes expected forward flow qualitatively. This view provides no velocity, pressure or Doppler measurements.
