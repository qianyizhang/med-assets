# Doppler and measurements

Open **Doppler & measurements** in a cardiac window and choose a check. The workbench pairs physical measurements from the beating heart with separately prescribed Doppler examples. The acquired reference panel remains available for comparison. [Browser captures](review.webp)

| Check | Inputs and calculation |
| --- | --- |
| Linear measurements | Freeze the current B-mode section and place two calipers by mouse or keyboard. The probe depth and raster scale convert pixel spacing to millimetres. PLAX saves LV diameters and wall thicknesses at their required phases. These support fractional shortening, relative wall thickness and the linear LV mass estimate. Saving a mid-systolic LVOT diameter also updates the outflow calculations for the session. |
| Biplane volumes | Intersect the native LV or LA blood pool with the neutral A4C and A2C planes. Divide each annulus-to-tip axis into 20 parts and intersect the endocardial borders perpendicularly. The paired diameters define elliptical disk areas. Disk thickness uses the longer long axis divided by 20. Sum volumes in mL. Compare modeled LV ED and ES for EF. Index LA ES volume to the entered example body surface area. |
| Right heart | RV-focused ED and ES areas give FAC. The displacement of the lateral tricuspid annular intersection along its ED–ES direction supplies a modeled M-mode track and TAPSE. Trace the RA at ES for area, single-plane disk volume and volume index. |
| Longitudinal strain | Track the same LV endocardial material points from ED through the beat in the three apical planes. Each wall contributes three equal ED arc-length segments. The graph shows the per-plane mean and reports the equally weighted end-systolic mean over 18 segments. This exercise uses modeled endocardial motion. Clinical speckle tracking adds a defined myocardial layer and tracking-quality review. |
| Color flow | A central forward-flow field follows moving chamber or vessel rings. The active AV and outlet valves gate modeled inflow and ejection. Venous return has a separate phasic profile. Project each sample onto the probe ray, apply the chosen Nyquist limit, clip to the actual lumen contours and mask intraluminal muscle. |
| PW, CW and tissue Doppler | [Protocol definitions](protocol.js) specify the site, units, ideal beam orientation and envelope. Beam obliquity introduces cosine loss. PW and tissue gates lose their target when moved laterally beyond it. CW samples along the beam and lacks depth selection. The cursor and manual time calipers support peak inspection and velocity–time integration. |
| Derived hemodynamics | E/A compares inflow peaks. E / mean e′ combines mitral E with the two annular e′ sites. LVOT area × VTI gives stroke volume. Model rate gives cardiac output. Aortic continuity uses LVOT and aortic VTIs. The simplified Bernoulli relation gives instantaneous gradients from m/s, with the mean taken over ejection. Conditional TR sampling adds an explicitly assumed RA pressure for an RVSP example. |
| Respiratory IVC | A separately prescribed six-second example varies the IVC diameter. Changing the inspiratory diameter updates the collapse fraction, (expiration − inspiration) / expiration, independently of the cardiac clock. |

The spectra use independent illustrative amplitudes with an adjustable LVOT diameter and example body surface area. The resulting values describe the exercise. Mass-conserving hemodynamics, scanner RF acquisition, clinical disease grading and automatic measurements on external references are outside its scope.

The comprehensive adult protocol supplies the window and Doppler site conventions. Chamber, right-heart, diastolic and strain publications describe the measurement methods and clinical interpretation limits. [Adult TTE](https://www.asecho.org/guideline/comprehensive-tte-in-adults/) · [Chamber quantification](https://www.asecho.org/guideline/cardiac-chamber-quantification-by-echo-in-adults/) · [Right heart](https://www.asecho.org/guideline/right-heart-in-adults-pulmonary-hypertension/) · [Diastolic function](https://www.asecho.org/guideline/left-ventricular-diastolic-function-by-echo/) · [Strain](https://www.asecho.org/guideline/clinical-applications-of-strain-echocardiography/)

From this directory, run `node verify-measure.mjs` for unit, phase, flow, sign, disk, strain and Doppler checks. Run `node verify-ui.mjs 'http://127.0.0.1:8887/cardiac/?view=a4c'` in the gallery Playwright environment to exercise the assembled study. An optional second argument captures the live controls and plotted surfaces.
