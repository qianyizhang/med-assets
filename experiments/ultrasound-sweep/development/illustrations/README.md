# Workbench requirement illustrations

The workbench uses one ImageGen thumbnail per wholly missing asset. The gallbladder wall has a separate context illustration for the paired inspection demo. All eight images were generated with the built-in ImageGen tool and resized to WebP for local display. Use the visible AI label and the caption from the [illustration registry](../assets-illustrations.js) wherever these images appear. The neck-space illustration resembles a scan and is synthetic.

## Generation prompts

Each prompt combines its prefix and subject.

**A** A single clean educational medical illustration on warm ivory, textbook cutaway, no lettering or symbols or embedded text. Muted natural colors, the requested structure emphasized in warm amber, centered with generous margins, legible at small thumbnail scale. For an illustration of anatomy requested for future 3D work.

**B** Paint ONE hand-rendered, full-color medical atlas spot illustration on warm ivory paper. SINGLE object, centered, NO text anywhere, NO letters, no diagrams with captions, no callout lines. Clean colored-pencil and gouache, muted terracotta and cream, flat even daylight, soft modeling of surfaces, a restrained teal highlight for relevant structures. This is artwork for a concept thumbnail, not a medical scan. NEVER show grayscale sonography, pixels, a black field, calipers, measurement scales, a monitor, overlays, legends or any typographic labeling.
