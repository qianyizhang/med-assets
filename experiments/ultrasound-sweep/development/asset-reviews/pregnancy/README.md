## Evidence and limits

The isolated neutral views use the same orthographic view, crop, light, and material within each comparison. The reverse pair changes camera and light direction. The grayscale pair removes hue. Native stage figures provide a same-source reference for unmodified parts and context for interpreted case variations. Distance values compare finite samples at vertices, edge midpoints, and face centroids in both directions. Silhouette differences use a 672 × 480 raster. Geometry identities hash the displayed position and index arrays.

Chrome confirmed identical evidence-frame indices and B-mode overlay pixels across 14 unique matching case chapters. The existing CPU suite passed all 197 pregnancy chapters. The review covers the two normal mid-stage targets, early embryo and ectopic yolk sac, normal and increased-NT first-stage fetal exteriors, the abnormal mid-stage exterior, membrane and cord, normal and low-growth late-stage fetal exteriors, shared placentas and cords by stage, and the sampled mid-normal pocket boundary.

Lower form grades remain attached to the increased-NT and wall-defect exteriors, the analytic membrane, the interpreted low-growth exterior, and the sampled fluid boundary. The boundaryʼs isolated fidelity remains unrated. Separate limited fetal exteriors, other case-specific fluid pockets, and incidental context surfaces remain outside the assessed set. Genuine same-finding anatomical references would be needed to qualify the affected neck, abdominal wall, growth contour, and tissue-interface finish at closer views. Specialist anatomical and clinical review remains open.
