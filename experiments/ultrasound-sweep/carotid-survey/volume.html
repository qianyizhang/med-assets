Volume source

The grayscale voxels come from the recorded simulated B-mode images and their calibrated probe poses. Each voxel samples a recorded section or the space between adjacent sections. The NRRD uses right–anterior–superior coordinates in millimetres and stores unobserved voxels as −1.
