# TAQ-024 · Compare native gland extents and posterior thyroid anatomy

**Owner** · Endocrine asset maintainer. The teaching-asset campaign lead owns this follow-up and its acceptance.

**Current use** · Localize four posterior positions in a single atlas example.

**Why form stays at 2** · Oriented ellipsoids preserve the four native centers and upper/lower dimensions (long axes 4.70/4.45 mm). At the posterior and oblique fields they reproduce the same bilateral mirror shapes and unresolved thyroid projection. Equal-size markers also erase upper/lower extents. Neither supplies specimen morphology.

**Next action** · Acquire a rights-cleared same-specimen posterior view with separately annotated glands and thyroid projection. Compare upper and lower extents at the native gland centers before replacing the atlas example.

**Acceptance** · Select a rights-cleared posterior specimen with its individual glands and central thyroid projection annotated in a shared frame. Test whether distinct contours improve localization while preserving the supported upper/lower extents.
