# TAQ-005 — Review body, cardiovascular, digestive and endocrine assets

[Teaching asset review campaign](../../campaigns/teaching-asset-review.md)

Assess the 24 exact [starting files](../../../docs/gallery/maintained-models.md#body--support) from Body & support, Cardiovascular, Digestive and Endocrine. Work within source-supported orientation, regional localization, exterior recognition, visible paths and authored relationships. Use sampled motion for the animated atlas and exercise the native Workshop only for the selected learner task.

Capture paired reimport views where current evidence is missing. A generic overview does not resolve landmark anatomy, selected occlusion or a claimed connection. Preserve known gaps in source registration, cutaway interpretation, chamber connections and medical validation.

## Result

[Exact assessments and evidence](../../../reviews/teaching-assessments/2026-11-22-body-cardiovascular-digestive-endocrine/README.md) account for 24/24 starting files. Eight reached at least 3 on all axes for the stated task. Keep the review scope at the individual task and exact file. Two matched teaching improvements restore the [gallbladder cut rim](../../../reviews/teaching-assessments/2026-11-22-body-cardiovascular-digestive-endocrine/views/12-isolation.webp) and [stomach rugae pattern](../../../reviews/teaching-assessments/2026-11-22-body-cardiovascular-digestive-endocrine/views/17-isolation.webp) through documented mutually exclusive visibility. Canonical GLB payloads and recommendation selection are preserved.

| Finding | Disposition |
| --- | --- |
| Source exterior obscures the separate authored gallbladder and stomach sections | Use the matched static views and Blender selection instructions now. The Anatomy Gallery owner can adopt a mutually exclusive preset only after checking the isolated states and the learner's normal reset path. |
| Standalone parathyroids omit their thyroid landmark | Teach the posterior relationship with the existing same-source context derivative and its annotated plate. Keep the maintained glands-only model scoped as a selectable target set. |
| Case s0189 thyroid bridge reads, but reverse ridges remain prominent | Retain the current exterior for bridge recognition. The thyroid package owner would need registered source contour interpretation before adopting a smoothing change. |
| Tall body outline and dense bowel meshes have unmeasured economy | Keep their exact exports. A future reduced-body test must protect fingers, toes and waist, while reduced-bowel tests must protect the taenia edges and the proximal small-bowel bridge. |
| The opaque animated heart makes deformation subtle and conceals leaflet movement | Use fixed-camera contour samples, and keep valve timing with the separate animated cutaway. Repeat interaction checks in the actual Library player when making a browser claim. |
