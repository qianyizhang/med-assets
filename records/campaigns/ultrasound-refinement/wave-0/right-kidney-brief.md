**Packet tolerances.** These are engineering comparison limits tied to the 1.5 mm source spacing. Freeze the independently marked reference endpoints, planes and sampling procedure before testing either CT version. Globally and in each protected plane, require p95 ≤1.5 mm and maximum ≤3 mm in both directions. Require each protected pole and the delivered endpoint to agree with the independently marked reference within 1.5 mm, with total chord error ≤1.5 mm and saved text agreeing with the displayed chord to its stated rounding. Require the first skin hit along the beam within 1.5 mm, consistent with the existing case-geometry check. Require an oriented closed outer shell with no unexplained tunnels, nonmanifold edges, self-intersections or degenerate faces. A same-connectivity refinement permits zero new reversed normals. For a lighter candidate, require at most one display pixel of turning-silhouette departure from the accepted shape at teaching zoom and preserve all geometric gates. Numerical compliance alone leaves teaching readability open. The reviewer must see and distinguish both poles and read the chord on the actual saved frame.

**Keep baseline** if an independently checked reuse or refinement produces no observable benefit for this action and the schematic still teaches the intended modeled measurement. **Hold pending evidence** for missing source or placement, a clipped pole, misregistered endpoints, a failed protected or topology gate, a false implication about the sinus, an identity mismatch, or a broken saved route. Keep the separate source review with its current task either way. **Adopt for the named task** only after independent lead review of the running chapter and its matched evidence. Apply axis scores only to the witnessed use. Workbench **Qualified** requires four evidenced 4/4 scores. This packet covers the outer contour only.
