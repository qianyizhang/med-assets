# Oral spaces / 口腔空间关系

Independent EN and 中文 coronal teaching cutaways. The vestibule lies lateral to teeth/gingiva, oral cavity proper medial; they communicate, not sealed compartments. Simplified mucosal reflections lead toward the alveolar/gingival region—not directly from cheek to palate.

冠状面教学示意：口腔前庭位于牙齿／牙龈外侧，固有口腔位于内侧；两者相通。黏膜反折经牙槽／牙龈区域，不是从颊直接接到硬腭。

## Why this diagram exists

- **Outcome** · A separate 2D aid for vestibule, gingiva, roof and floor relationships. The existing head SVG labels mouth and tongue at head scale.
- **3D findings** · Detached buccal rods and a floating palate slab made the original cavity unreadable. Thin cheek panels improved its silhouette. Adding same-frame teeth and tongue still left poor crown/root presentation and a plate-like roof. Direct cheek-to-palate joins omit the alveolar/gingival region and vestibule.
- **Scope** · The original 3D mouth remains failed and needs revision. [Independent 2D review](../../../../reviews/oral_spaces/2026-10-26-r01/INDEPENDENT-REVIEW.md).
- **中文** · 原始颊部圆杆与腭板分离，薄片修订仍不足以形成清楚的口腔整体。颊部直接连到硬腭会省略牙龈、牙槽区域和口腔前庭。本图只补足二维教学关系，原三维模型仍需修改。
