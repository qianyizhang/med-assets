# 3D Anatomy Workshop teaching edition

Use the compact native Workshop to locate the heart and lower thoracic space. Use the matched CT/label cuts to interpret the rounded inferior margins at gross scale.

The complete editable native payload drops from 17,571,351 to 13,596,421 bytes (22.6%). The earlier frozen mesh trial lost required controls. All 286,402 evaluated default triangles and the alternate native states survive in this edition. Both matched selections are pixel-identical.

Anterior and posterior views retain both lower contours. At two native coronal CT sections through the largest lower-quarter discrepancies, the sloping rims track the air-to-soft-tissue boundaries. The display rounds the label edges, by up to 2.94 mm left and 2.91 mm right in the sampled vertex-to-label-vertex check. Teach the lower contour at this scale.
