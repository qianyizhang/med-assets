# Dental teaching plan

Consolidated from [#217](https://github.com/qianyizhang/med-assets/issues/217)
and [#228](https://github.com/qianyizhang/med-assets/issues/228), 2026-11-06.
**Deferred, not delivered.** No geometry, source acquisition or publication is
scheduled by this plan. Resume a bounded slice only on an explicit request.

## Keep the useful starting points

- Preserve the accepted [schematic cleaning demo (#218)](https://github.com/qianyizhang/med-assets/issues/218)
  and [caries shooting-script proposal](../../lessons/dental-animation/caries-restoration/README.md).
  Neither is a realistic procedure or clinical validation.
- Preserve the [FDI36 surface experiment](../../experiments/dental/fdi36-surface-cleanup/README.md)
  as noncanonical research. Its intact high-detail baseline remains unaccepted;
  the shadow-attribution correction does not justify another geometry revision.
- Existing BP3D exteriors support a native-frame whole-set checkpoint. Coarse
  crowns/gingival boundaries, prepared LMU specimens and root/support teaching
  geometry do not automatically supply an intact restorative or subgingival baseline.

## Two bounded outcomes

| Outcome | Smallest useful delivery | Prerequisite |
|---|---|---|
| Caries → restoration | One modular adult molar: intact, localized cavitated lesion, illustrative prepared state and separate restoration; matched state views and a short shooting script | Licensed, sufficiently detailed intact baseline; documented lesion site/extent and preparation state |
| Cleaning explanation | One source-qualified tooth/gingival segment with independent localized deposits and a generic dimensioned tool; continuous approach, removal and withdrawal preview | Appropriate tooth/gingival context, deposit/site references and one explicit teaching target |

The old BP3D cleaning baseline is unsuitable for a realistic gingival-margin or
subgingival close-up. Keep that demo schematic. Do not register unrelated internals
or gingiva by eye, fabricate measured tissue thickness, or copy lesions across a set.

## Resume in this order

1. Qualify existing assets before new acquisition. Record actual licence, version,
   hashes, units, native relationships, preparation state and measured versus
   authored features. Reject an unsuitable baseline; stop if access/rights or
   morphology evidence is missing.
2. Select one outcome and define its shot, editable components and exclusions.
   Reuse the generic tool where suitable; no speculative instrument catalogue.
3. Build one common-frame pair/sequence while preserving unaffected source shape.
   Verify export/reimport, repeat/reset/interruption, sampled motion/clearance and
   deposit/state progression. Sampling is not continuous contact proof.
4. Review matched overview/detail and decoded motion. Keep technical, visual,
   dental/anatomical, runtime and publication acceptance separate. No clinical
   promotion, invented procedural parameters or automatic asset/Site publication.

The [component specification](README.md) and [set-review contract](set-review.md)
own detailed semantics and acceptance gates; do not duplicate them here. Preserve
source, masters, licences, failed trials and exact review identities. Deliver one
reviewable slice before expanding scope.

A coherent whole-mouth checkpoint can reuse the 28 BP3D exteriors and two shared
gingiva surfaces, with source-to-FDI mapping and contact review. Filling missing
adult slots, qualifying primary teeth and adding internal/condition families are
later work, gated by evidence—not target counts or mirrored/scaled substitutes.
