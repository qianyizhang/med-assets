# Refine a scan-derived teaching surface

Refine a CT/MRI segmentation or extracted surface whenever slice terraces, spurious holes or excess triangles hurt the learner view or delivery budget (for example, turning the `23,156`-triangle terraced s1233 right kidney into a smooth `4,800`-triangle teaching exterior with both poles and medial recess intact). Use the scan as the anatomical reference for placement, proportion, orientation and protected landmarks. See [Visual and geometric quality target](visual-quality-target.md) for what to inspect and [Review a teaching asset](asset-quality.md) for scoring.

## Before shaping

Pin the learner action, closest intended view, protected landmarks, reference and tolerances before trialing a change. State the expected components, shells, openings, lumens and boundaries for the task. Name any uncertain features the scan cannot resolve. Keep the original volume, segmentation and delivered mesh identities distinct. Record laterality, units, source terms, voxel spacing and the `rasMmFromVoxel` affine. When only a mask or mesh survives, call it the **case reference** and bound claims accordingly. External specimens or atlases can inform shape interpretation. Use the case reference to place this individual's anatomy.

Derive the physical slice direction from the acquisition orientation. For a regular slice stack, the normal is perpendicular to both in-plane directions mapped into physical space. Record orientation and spacing separately for the acquisition and working grid. In a reoriented or resampled mask, identify the direction of the artifact against the source acquisition before choosing a smoothing axis. When the original orientation is unavailable, record the uncertainty and test local changes in matched sections.

## CT-to-mesh refinement pipeline

1. **Inspect the label before cleanup.** Remove components only after identifying them as noise in the source sections. For an enclosed background cavity confirmed as an artifact, try `binary_fill_holes` and inspect which voxels changed. A through-tunnel connects to the exterior, so it needs a localized, evidenced repair if it is spurious. Preserve anatomical lumens, cuts and uncertain openings. A finer isotropic grid can help when the smoother needs sub-voxel sampling. It interpolates the source and leaves its acquisition resolution unchanged.
2. **Smooth in signed distance field (SDF) space where needed.** Calculate distances and specify kernel widths in physical units. For slice terraces, test stronger smoothing along the physical acquisition slice normal. Use less smoothing near protected poles, thin walls, clefts and hilums. For a source-supported cap correction, check the end contour and span together. If smoothing changes enclosed volume, try a bounded level-set adjustment and remeasure protected sections. For touching organs, inspect contact and clearance together. Joint multi-label smoothing ([3D Slicer](https://slicer.readthedocs.io/en/latest/user_guide/modules/segmenteditor.html#smoothing) or VTK `vtkSurfaceNets3D`) can help with shared interfaces.
3. **Extract and verify topology.** Use `marching_cubes(..., allow_degenerate=False)` on the SDF or a multi-label extractor such as `vtkSurfaceNets3D`. Check each component's edges, winding and consistency with the expected anatomy. For a connected, closed, orientable manifold shell, compute `χ = V − E + F` and `g = (2 − χ) / 2`. A single shell with no handles has `χ = 2`. Genus counts handles, including intended ones. Inspect their location against source planes. For a part with several shells or intentional open edges, check the shells or boundary loops individually. Use signed volume on closed shells with the winding appropriate to the represented material, including cavity walls.
4. **Fair or sculpt only where the source supports a correction.** For residual ripples, try Taubin or Windowed Sinc fairing and measure the contour afterward. If editing in [Blender](../WORKFLOW.md#blender-workflow), use a replayable recipe and inspect the protected sections after shaping. Check for inverted faces after geometry moves. The [liver pretrial](../../records/campaigns/ultrasound-refinement/pretrial/README.md) found reversals after a one-axis height-field shift.
5. **Trial a lighter mesh when the delivered density warrants it.** Use feature-aware quadratic error metric (QEM) decimation such as Blender `Decimate` Collapse or [mesh_contraction.py](../../lib/med_assets/mesh_contraction.py). For closed shapes, a voxel remesh followed by `QuadriFlow` can be a retopology option. Recheck topology and cuts after either operation. Thin walls and tight clefts can erode or bridge. Recompute normals for the verified topology and judge density at the closest teaching view. [Soft mesh budgets](asset-quality.md#soft-mesh-budgets)

## Common caveats and fixes

| Defect | Root cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Slice staircasing or terraces | Direct extraction from a coarse mask | Trial local smoothing aligned to the acquisition normal. Check the contour and span. |
| Volume shrinkage or eroded tips | Global smoothing or strong Gaussian blur | Trial narrower polar kernels or volume-aware extraction, then measure both volume and tip position. |
| Flat end-slice cap | Abrupt first or last occupied slice | Check the source for plausible continuation. When supported, round locally and measure cap contours and span. |
| Unexplained through-tunnel | Partial-volume loss in a thin region, or intended anatomy | Localize it in the source planes and compare with the expected topology. Close it locally only if supported and record the edit. |
| Erased cleft, hilum or thin wall | Global smoothing or coarse `QuadriFlow`/QEM | Narrow local SDF kernel + vertex-group protection; inspect cut planes before/after. |
| Reversed triangle normals | One-axis vertex push or aggressive unconstrained smoothing | Trial 3D field smoothing or fairing. Check orientation after clean export import. |
| Touching-organ gaps or overlap | Independent heavy smoothing of adjacent masks | Joint multi-label smoothing (`vtkSurfaceNets3D` / Slicer) and assembled contact check. |

## Decide whether to ship

Clean-import the candidate export (`.glb` or `.mesh`) and the exact previously delivered binary when comparing a replacement. Pin the delivered paths and digests. Capture both at the same camera, physical scale, crop, view size, lighting and material. Include the lesson state and disclose any deliberate differences. Verify part identities, laterality, placement, topology, face quality, winding and neighbor contacts on the exported geometry.

Compare both exports against the same pinned case reference. Use bidirectional `p95` and maximum distances globally and for the protected landmarks in their own scan planes. Add enclosed volume change when the representation supports it. Use the same sampling and tolerances for both exports.

- For blunt caps, measure cap contour distance and chord or principal-axis span. In the [Wave 1 kidney trial](../../records/campaigns/ultrasound-refinement/wave-1/README.md), the extrema slid about 3 mm along the contour while the saved chord changed by less than 1 mm.
- Locate the largest section errors on the witness. Compare the full and clipped contours when a sector edge could affect the result.
- Compare a lighter trial at the intended zoom. Record the largest visible change, including at thin rims and turning silhouettes, and the savings in triangles and bytes.

Retain the matched overview, decisive close-up in neutral clay and finished lighting, and the reference section overlay. Record editorial edits and source limits. Have an independent reviewer attempt the named learner action on both deliveries and examine the protected features before making a disposition. Record the observation for each score under the [rubric](asset-quality.md). For ultrasound, follow the [campaign acceptance checks](../ULTRASOUND_REFINEMENT_SPEC.md#qualification-and-acceptance) for saved acquisition and review binding.
