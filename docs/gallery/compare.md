# Compare PRD

**Outcome.** A reviewer can explore a difference immediately and turn the experiment into a citable review when there is a specific finding.

## Two moments

**Explore.** Open identified revisions or presets side by side. Match pose, framing, light and material where the question requires it. Label separate coordinate frames or independent scales. A neutral material or blind A/B view can help isolate the source of a difference.

Any two models can be inspected side by side. Enable a spatial overlay or a displacement measurement only when the models have known relative alignment and physical scale. For an unaligned pair, keep both views available and explain what information the overlay requires.

**Save a review.** Record the question, exact inputs, preset revisions, decisive images, reviewer, date, finding and limits. Attach a scored assessment when the rubric and evidence apply. The review owns its decision; the library and lesson can display it in scope.

## Migrated verification studies

A migrated Anatomy Verification study earns a saved review page when it has identified inputs, evidence and a finding or documented limitation. Preserve cited candidates, sections, images and reviewer attribution. A single-revision inspection can compare a model with source evidence. Link teaching-only material to Use Cases and exact exports to Asset Library. Both sides of an interactive 3D comparison use the shared scene presentation with their own pinned presets. Historical images open in their recorded form.

## Teaching refinements

In the local Gallery, open **Compare → Teaching asset refinements** for the [reviewed set](../../records/campaigns/teaching-asset-refinement.md). The asset selector groups cases by learner risk. Open the refinement evidence at full size or compare it alongside the original. A shareable link identifies the control export by digest. Score changes apply to that export and task. A separately assessed candidate has its own column. Verified local candidates offer downloads and recovery instructions.

## Acceptance example

Starting at s1233 r05, compare the inferior adrenal fold with r04 under the same camera. Switch both sides to neutral, then open the matched case section. A saved review reopens on those revisions after a later model appears. A visitor can recover which side, slice and view support the finding.
