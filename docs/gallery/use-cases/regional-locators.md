# Regional locators

A learner can use regional context to locate the target, then read its labeled close-up at the source's own orientation and scale.

Cardiovascular links to the aortic arch and pulmonary-vein stops, Nervous and senses to the brainstem source cut, and Reproductive to the ovary and tubal end. The arch and vein use separate sources. Give the composite labels room to read and keep the matched views together inside the original plates. The brain lesson opens the reviewed front comparison and links the source package for additional views.

Reproductive left pickup and the Regional locators ovary stop open the same [bilingual lesson](../../../lessons/ovary-tubal-context/README.md). Each returns to its originating collection. The lesson moves from pelvic orientation to the interpreted HRA close-ups, marks the frame and scale change and offers the original contours under **Compare with source**.

Serve lossless WebP at the original dimensions, with the retained PNG as fallback. If both fail, keep the teaching text, scale notes, source and review links, and any other available plate.
