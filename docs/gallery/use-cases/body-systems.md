# Body Systems

A learner can orient in a body system, find the structure that answers a question, then inspect the source or an exact Library revision. Depending on the system, the action is to trace a route, locate a junction, isolate a structure or compare poses.

## Collection and views

The direct Body Systems shortcut opens the eleven systems under Use Cases at `/#/use-cases/systems`. Each system card gives a representative view, the assembly's placement status and its teaching question or function. **System assembly** opens first. **Asset detail** selects a named relationship or view, with a direct `?detail=` bookmark. Keep return navigation to the collection, assembly or originating detail as appropriate. [Route contract](../routes-and-api.md#page-links)

Serve reviewed paired plates in the ordinary Gallery. Each pair gives the complementary angles needed for the question, with named views, a color legend and a scale or placement note where needed. Keep scope limits beside the teaching material. An independently sourced or enlarged detail has its own frame and scale. A source-frame assembly can still have incomplete coverage or joins. Keep illustrative layout, authored routes and same-case placement explicit. Link to the related Library category and to an exact revision when a stop depends on it. ## Linked lessons

- **Respiratory.** One Breath links its route step to the Respiratory airway sequence. Its cutaway lets the learner trace the inner wall into the left main bronchus. Airway Review uses the open shell to identify the proximal mouth and both distal outlets. Mechanics and gas exchange retain their own questions and scale cues. [One Breath](../../../lessons/one-breath/README.md) · [Lesson overlap review](../../../records/campaigns/organ-and-lesson-overlaps.md#lesson-decisions)
- **Cardiovascular.** The synthetic cardiac slice shows the same reference-phase plane in labeled color, CT-like grayscale and MRI-like grayscale. The source package also contains a separate ultrasound-style fan and records its own plane. [Cardiac imaging package](../../../heart/synthetic-imaging/beating-heart/imaging/README.md)
- **Skeletal, nervous and muscular.** The right elbow and shoulder exercises keep shared bones visible across layers. At the elbow they orient the switch from capsule to ulnar nerve. At the shoulder the glenoid and scapular spine orient the switch to the cuff. The reveal adds anterior and posterior views when one angle hides a muscle. [Right-arm lessons](../../../records/work-items/items/AG-GAL-029-right-arm-part-lessons.md)
- **Skeletal.** Open the second costal cartilage and L3–L4 exterior disc stops from Body Systems or Use Cases. The front and side plates retain the neighboring bones. Oblique and neutral-gray views show the same assemblies with different materials. Each stop links to its exact dated Library export. [Scoped review](../../../reviews/skeletal_teaching/2026-10-23/record.json)
- **Urinary and reproductive.** The pelvic exercises live in the existing left vesical and female reproductive context stops. Hide the bladder dome to expose the ureteral entries, base and neck. Hide the bladder and rectum to isolate the cervix and vaginal exterior. Both mark the switch to the HRA atlas and preserve the original system plates and neighboring stops. Reproductive left pickup and [Regional locators](regional-locators.md) share one ovary lesson. [Pelvic exercises](../../../records/work-items/items/AG-GAL-031-pelvic-part-lessons.md)

Keep distinct tasks when the same structures explain a different relationship. [Lesson overlap review](../../../records/campaigns/organ-and-lesson-overlaps.md#lesson-decisions)
