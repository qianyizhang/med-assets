# Use Cases PRD

**Outcome.** A learner can answer a question using a sequence of authored views. Body Systems is the first collection. Additional collections can organize their lessons differently and reuse the same asset revisions.

## Lesson responsibilities

A lesson names its question and owns the progression from overall assembly to asset to part when those scales help. At each stop, identify the target, its relation to its neighbors and any deliberate color, visibility or scale change. Each interactive 3D stop embeds the shared scene using a scene preset. The lesson owns interactions such as choosing a cardiac phase or advancing an airway animation, and passes the corresponding state to the scene. Static diagrams, sections and video can stand alone. Keep a poster and explanatory caption available when interactive 3D is unavailable.

Give a substantial teaching experience such as Body & Heart its own use case page or collection on the Site. Fold smaller lessons into a relevant stop when they answer the same question. Use the single **Experiments & Archive** collection for a distinct experiment that still has public teaching or historical value. Retire a duplicate public presentation after preserving its unique evidence and resolving its published links.

Use a continuous zoom when the detail has a known position and scale in the overview. For a separately sourced detail without that alignment, mark its anatomical region on the overview, then cut to a labeled view with its own source and scale. If scale is unknown, say so.

When the [Asset Library](asset-library.md) recommendation changes, offer each affected lesson author a [comparison](compare.md) between the lesson’s pinned revision and the recommended one. Open both in the lesson’s camera, colors and part selection so the author can judge the teaching landmark. The lesson keeps its pinned revision until its author explicitly adopts the update.

Each switch preserves the shared anatomical frame, camera and scale. In 3D it also preserves the learner’s rotation and zoom. A matched still covers each layer when a model is unavailable. The point and answer state last only for the current visit.

## Acceptance examples

In the endocrine lesson, locate both glands relative to their kidneys in the regional view. Open the right-gland stop, isolate the right adrenal and inspect its inferior rim. Returning to the overview restores the context and the learner’s place. Opening the asset page identifies the same s1233 revision. A separately sourced atlas example carries a related-study label. The lesson’s readability assessment names the task that was tested.

If s1233 r06 becomes Recommended while the endocrine lesson uses r05, opening and closing the comparison leaves learners on r05. The author can inspect the inferior rim in the lesson’s close-up and adopt r06 if the rim still reads. The updated lesson then opens the r06 asset and its matching preset.
