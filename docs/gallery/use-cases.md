# Use Cases PRD

**Outcome.** A learner can answer a question using a sequence of authored views. Body Systems is the first collection. Additional collections can organize their lessons differently and reuse the same asset revisions.

## Lesson responsibilities

A lesson names its question and owns the progression from overall assembly to asset to part when those scales help. At each stop, identify the target, its relation to its neighbors and any deliberate color, visibility or scale change. Each stop references its assets and staged preset. Keep a static view and explanatory caption available when interactive 3D is unavailable.

Use a continuous zoom when the detail has a known position and scale in the overview. For a separately sourced detail without that alignment, mark its anatomical region on the overview, then cut to a labeled view with its own source and scale. If scale is unknown, say so.

When the [Asset Library](asset-library.md) recommendation changes, offer each affected lesson author a [comparison](compare.md) between the lesson’s pinned revision and the recommended one. Open both in the lesson’s camera, colors and part selection so the author can judge the teaching landmark. The lesson keeps its pinned revision until its author explicitly adopts the update.

## Acceptance examples

In the endocrine lesson, locate the left gland above and medial to its kidney in the regional view. Isolate the gland and inspect its edge. Returning to the overview restores the context and the learner’s place. Opening the asset page identifies the same s1233 revision. A separately sourced atlas example carries a related-study label. The lesson’s readability assessment names the task that was tested.

If s1233 r06 becomes Recommended while the endocrine lesson uses r05, opening and closing the comparison leaves learners on r05. The author can inspect the inferior rim in the lesson’s close-up and adopt r06 if the rim still reads. The updated lesson then opens the r06 asset and its matching preset.
