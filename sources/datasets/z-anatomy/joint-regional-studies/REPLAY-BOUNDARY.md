# Bounded preservation, not native reconstruction
The historical migration preserves three exact r01 GLBs and selected public WebP
presentation evidence. It retains member hashes and the pinned source URL.
The [shared right-arm recipe](../../../../models/joints/recipes/z-right-arm/README.md)
builds the currently assessed r02 elbow and shoulder selections from that member.
The r01 images retain their exact historical bindings. The original PNGs, review
Blend scenes, evaluated OBJ snapshots, author build scripts and full authoring
toolchain are outside this preservation record.

Geometry AND derived renders: CC BY-SA 4.0, with upstream historical BodyParts3D attribution retained. Camera contract converts Blender Z-up coordinates to exported Three Y-up; do not rotate the GLB again. No BP3D/CT registration. Static reviewer acceptance is scoped; no new runtime, clinical, physics or whole-joint pass.
