The [pinned upstream repository](https://github.com/Z-Anatomy/Models-of-human-anatomy/tree/c08bde5325e35a46404d86a6bd458c484297cc2d)
is public. Exact archive, license and extracted scene identities are in
[provenance.json](provenance.json). No account, payment or source-script execution
was needed. The archive remains external and is not redistributed here.

Selected geometry and source-derived renders retain **CC BY-SA 4.0**. Adaptations
must retain attribution, change descriptions and the same license. The source
inventory lists no testis-specific exception; this is not an independent
per-object rights audit. No repository-wide license or new license for authored
scripts/prose is inferred.

Changes: selective extraction of named native parts, evaluation of existing
modifiers/curves, illustrative materials and visibility. Source transform, scale
and anatomical uncertainty remain explicit. No modeled lumen, internal coiling,
clinical validation or measured tissue appearance is claimed.

Reference text consulted, with no third-party figure reproduced:

- [NCI SEER: Testes](https://www.training.seer.cancer.gov/anatomy/reproductive/male/testes.html)
- [NCI SEER: Duct system](https://www.training.seer.cancer.gov/anatomy/reproductive/male/duct.html)

These establish the broad testis/epididymis relationship, not the accuracy of
this particular mesh, its dimensions or its native laterality labels.
