## Source and measurement limits

- The s1233 NIfTI CT and labels have the same 265 × 265 × 401 voxel frame and 1.5 mm spacing. The recipe checks their content hashes and affine. It transforms clean-import vertices using the Workshop's documented RAS conversion. The one-sided upper bound uses the nearest vertex of a 0.5 label isosurface at every target-mesh vertex in the inferior or superior height quartile. These samples cover the affected bands. They do not bound complete continuous surfaces or establish fine tissue identity. Crops of the CT and label contours share their coordinates. Images and labels are credited to Jakob Wasserthal and the TotalSegmentator contributors, University Hospital Basel, under CC BY 4.0. ## Replay

The starting revision is `0bc388a5b48d65ddb53060840470164ff1c6ce6a`. The comparison uses Python 3.12, Pillow 11.3.0, NumPy 2.2.6, SciPy 1.16.1, nibabel 5.4.2, scikit-image 0.25.2, Zstandard CLI 1.5.7 and Blender 5.2.3 LTS Release Candidate `fab76e95306d`. Run at repository root with hydrated historical payloads. Supply the external source directory for case s1233 with `ct.nii.gz` and `segmentations/`, and the hash-verified Z-Anatomy `Startup.blend`. The recipes do not download the source archive or execute its scripts.

```sh
pack=reviews/teaching-assessments/reopen-paired
scratch=.artifacts/paired-replay
mkdir "$scratch"
python3.12 "$pack/build.py" "$scratch/editions"
cmp "$scratch/editions/build.json" "$pack/build.json"
blender --background --factory-startup --disable-autoexec --threads 4 --python-exit-code 1 \
  --python "$pack/render.py" -- "$scratch/editions" "$scratch/matched"
blender --background --factory-startup --disable-autoexec --threads 4 --python-exit-code 1 \
  --python "$pack/atlas.py" -- "$atlas_blend" "$scratch/atlas"
python3.12 "$pack/sections.py" "$s1233_directory/segmentations" "$scratch/atlas" "$scratch/matched" "$scratch/sections"
python3.12 "$pack/plates.py" "$scratch/matched" "$scratch/sections" "$scratch/plates"
```
