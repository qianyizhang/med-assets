# Why this aid exists

The original 3D mouth uses detached capped buccal rods and a floating palate slab. Replacing rods with thin panels improved a local silhouette but did not create a coherent mouth. Adding its original same-frame dental/tongue assets retained poor crowns/root presentation and plate-like roof. Neither 3D experiment is promoted. Direct cheek-to-hard-palate welding would bypass the gingiva/alveolar region and vestibule and is not a valid shortcut.

This separate 2D coronal teaching concept shows the missing relationships without pretending to recover accurate 3D anatomy. The original 3D mouth remains failed/Needs revision; quality-count delta0.

3D口腔仍需修改：原始颊部圆杆与腭板分离；改成薄片仍不足以形成有用的整体。把颊黏膜直接焊接到硬腭会省略牙龈、牙槽区域和口腔前庭。本图只补足2D教学关系，不代表3D模型通过。
