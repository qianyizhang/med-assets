# Failure → revision → limits

The original 24short-root pairs plus 7 cauda pairs were nominally 31 pairs, but the lower 14strands shared almost the same origins and collapsed visually into 2 tails. The conus was a separate overlapping ellipsoid. A count did not establish useful anatomy.

R01 introduced a projected root lattice and was rejected. R02 improved lane order but 6 nonadjacent strand pairs had negative sampled clearance; fixed-depth origins could also float outside the tapering conus. R03 separates depth lanes without shrinking root radii and places starts inside the cord ellipse. Independent fresh-export screens then found no unrelated contacts in 5,151 tested component pairs; all 68 representative rootobjects contact the cord.

This still is a **representative teaching model**, not a31 pair reconstruction. The lesson is to verify attachments and crossings, not to add anatomical-sounding labels to a fishbone primitive or treat manifold geometry as clinical validation.

原模型虽然名义上有31对结构，但末端重叠成2条尾状束，计数并不等于关系正确。修订中先否决交叉网状布局，再纠正根间接近及圆锥旁悬空的起点；仅接受经过实际图像和导出检查的教学关系改善，不宣称完整解剖通过。
