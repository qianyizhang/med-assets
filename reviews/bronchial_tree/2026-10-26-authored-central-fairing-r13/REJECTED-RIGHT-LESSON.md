# Right-junction experiments excluded

R10/R11 diffusion retained a conspicuous cap pocket despite 0.5/0.75 mm movement bounds. R12 tapered a 4.8 mm construction cap to 1.2 mm: its matched rotated image exposed a new waist; closed sampled surface sections lost up to 40.3% area. It was rejected, not hidden as successful overlap cleanup. None of these geometries is included in r13. Lesson: topology success and removal of a lip do not justify narrowing the visible parent junction. The right defect remains in the deliverable.
