# Right median nerve — wrist and hand skeletal localization

This separate regional study places the selected median trunk and common/proper
palmar digital source groups against native arm, wrist and hand bones. It serves
[#164](https://github.com/qianyizhang/med-assets/issues/164), within collection #150;
the original schematic and merged ulnar/elbow study remain unchanged.

`right-median-wrist.glb`: **834,404 bytes**, **33 objects / 38,414 triangles**,
SHA-256 `6094265d2263f3b501f4e487ff7f859b83205bde99bca9312d48c26f29680c50`.
The V1 export is retained unchanged; V2 adds a separate bilingual teaching sheet.
All selected evaluated surfaces and effective world matrices survive. Source
hierarchy is flattened and display colors are illustrative; masters are untouched.

**Skeletal localization only.** The native flexor/retinaculum screen left unresolved
nerve occlusion and overlap candidates, so those relationships are excluded.
Omitting soft tissues does not resolve that source conflict. Palmar cutaneous and
recurrent/motor branches, complete skin territories, nerve continuity, tunnel
clearance, clinical, motion, physical-scale and browser-runtime validation are
outside this study. Curve endpoints are source extents, not validated origins or
terminations. Coarse source bone form and gaps remain.
