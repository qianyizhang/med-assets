# Right ulnar nerve and elbow bones

A separate four-part source-native teaching supplement: the right ulnar nerve
relative to the humerus, ulna and radius. The labelled sheet locates the medial
epicondyle and olecranon and supplies whole-arm context plus two detail views.
It does not replace `draft_peripheral_nerves` or complete issue 150.

`right-ulnar-elbow.glb`: **187,708 bytes**, SHA-256
`94a7bde3f8a2882cc632082301d7701a737a769e1d1afe0f55629ecac3013ac3`.
Four meshes, 4,720 exported vertices / 9,136 triangles. Curve seam duplication
accounts for the exported nerve's additional vertices. Native evaluated surfaces
and transforms are preserved; colors are illustrative.
