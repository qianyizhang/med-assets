# Right sciatic nerve: posterior hip locator

A separate four-part teaching study for [issue #166](https://github.com/qianyizhang/med-assets/issues/166):
the retained sciatic course relative to the right hip bone, sacrum and femur.
The lesson identifies the ischial-tuberosity and greater-trochanter regions with
whole-subset orientation and two hip details.
