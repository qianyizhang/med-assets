# Diaphragm teaching edition

Use the compact isolated diaphragm to compare dome heights and the source-bound insets to locate the projected caval opening and descending contours alongside L1.

Packing only the normals saves 77,624 bytes (11.1%) with all 38,868 triangles, four original boundary edges, dome crests and narrow rims intact. The maximum normal direction change was 0.00145 degrees. All three 640-pixel silhouettes match and the maximum channel difference was 1/255.

The neutral anterior, upper and lower views distinguish both domes. The same-atlas thoracic caval cross-section center falls inside the projected superior opening, 1.53 mm from its centroid. At the lower cut, the two descending contours flank the L1 body. Preserve these source relationships for the gross task. Rims and vessel profiles only partially coincide.
