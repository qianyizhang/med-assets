# Diaphragm · gross static source study

One reviewed study with two explicitly different export roles:
- `diaphragm_z_native_static.glb`: isolated primary study.
- `diaphragm_z_context_static.glb`: auxiliary partial same-source skeletal context, not a second accepted candidate.

Reviewer-inspected acceptance is limited to **gross static shape and regional localization**. The earlier schematic diaphragm and its animation remain unchanged and need revision. This is not a finished detailed anatomy model, breathing animation, clinical validation or browser-runtime pass.

## Important limits
Smooth source surfaces do not differentiate central tendon or muscle fibres. Unnamed openings and unexplained narrow projections remain; four source boundary edges are retained. Partial ribs/sternum/spine context omits cartilage and much of the thorax. Free rib ends do not prove attachments. Native Z-Anatomy coordinates are not registered to other atlas or patient data.
