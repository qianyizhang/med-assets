# Right rotator cuff muscle localization

One six-surface native-coordinate [GLB](right-rotator-cuff.glb): right humerus,
scapula, supraspinatus, infraspinatus, teres minor and subscapularis. Use the
[teaching sheet](../../../../reviews/joints/2026-10-30-rotator-cuff-r01/v2/teaching.png)
to locate the posterior muscle surfaces around the scapular spine and the
subscapularis surface on the opposing face. Whole-subset and close views explain
the humeral-shaft crop; the model retains the full selected bones.

This is a separate supplement. The accepted
[ligament/labrum study](../z-shoulder-r01/README.md) remains unchanged.
No origin/insertion marker has been converted into tissue. No complete attachment,
tendon continuity, bursae, cartilage, muscle action, motion, clinical validity,
physical-scale calibration or browser-runtime approval is claimed.

The [canonical inventory](inventory.json) binds the source, source surfaces and
effective native matrices, illustrative material colors, and exact GLB. Six meshes,
38,678 triangles, 703,536 bytes; SHA-256
`edf5c2fc5322adc1c75703143b3fba5a2be3ecd87d5559bce1441c1977a4eb30`.
Geometry and relative positioning are preserved; the source's Humerus.r has four
boundary edges and two nonmanifold edges. This is not a watertight mechanics model.
Source `.r` laterality is retained; no external-body registration was performed.
