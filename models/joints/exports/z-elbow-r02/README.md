# Right elbow capsule and ligaments

Locate the capsule relative to the right humerus, radius and ulna. Toggle the
capsule and humerus to inspect the annular and collateral ligaments in depth.
The selection keeps the static native pose and covers the selected joint surfaces.
Tendons, muscles, bursae and separate articular cartilage are outside its scope.
