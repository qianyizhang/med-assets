# Nail unit longitudinal half-section

Trace the thin nail plate from its buried proximal root, over the matrix and bed, to the free edge above the fingertip. The GLB opens with the plate seated. The two views with the plate lifted are optional teaching illustrations of the underlying surfaces.

This regional study derives its five named parts and local frame from the [r01 skin teaching block](../teaching-block-r01/README.md) and its [owning visual review](../../../reviews/skin/teaching-block-r01/README.md). The whole skin-and-nail teaching block remains available for orientation. The section exposes the same center plane and includes the retained far-side nail margin. Blender uses millimetres for design and stores geometry in metres. Blender +X is distal, +Y is away from the sagittal cut and +Z is dorsal. In glTF, +Y is dorsal and +Z points toward the cut. The study has no atlas registration or laterality.

## Form and evidence

- The proximal fold covers the root from above and the matrix supports it from below. The matrix transitions into a thin longitudinal bed directly beneath the plate. The cut face keeps these tissues separately selectable. [Fleckman et al., human nail microanatomy and Fig. 5](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3579226/)
- The plate crowns along both axes. Its thin shell rounds in plan toward the distal edge and passes beyond the end of the bed and surrounding tissue. The skin under the unsupported edge falls into the rounded fingertip profile. [Jung et al., fingernail curvature measurements](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4659990/)
- A brighter keratin material marks the exposed free edge within the same plate mesh. The tissue colours, enlarged matrix profile for legibility, widths and heights, section ends, retained lateral contour and colour boundary are authored teaching choices. The five meshes describe gross relations. The subtle far-side fold and hyponychial region are simplified in the skin silhouette. Cuticle, lunula, distal phalanx and separate histological layers are outside the study scope. [OpenStax, Anatomy and Physiology 2e §5.2](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/5-2-accessory-structures-of-the-skin)

No reference mesh, figure or texture is embedded. The original source attribution and distribution terms remain with r01. A redistribution licence for this geometry is unspecified.

## Local artifacts and replay

The [portable nail](nail.glb) and [gallery review](../../../reviews/body-systems/2026-11-20-surface-quality/README.md) preserve the exact model, measurements and seated and lifted comparisons. The editable Blender scene is generated by the same recipe. Rebuild from the repository root with Blender 5.2.3. Restore the original input through the [body-systems teaching backup](../../../sites/body-systems-survey/README.md#restore-the-media) when needed. Choose a new output path on each run. On this Mac, follow the repository's approval instruction for headless Blender.

```sh
recipe=models/skin/nail-study-r02
original=models/skin/teaching-block-r01/skin-block.glb
out=.artifacts/nail-study-replay
blender -b -t 1 --factory-startup --disable-autoexec --python-exit-code 1 -P "$recipe/build.py" -- "$out/build"
blender -b -t 1 --factory-startup --disable-autoexec --python-exit-code 1 -P "$recipe/check.py" -- "$original" "$out/build/nail.glb" "$out/metrics.json"
blender -b -t 4 --factory-startup --disable-autoexec --python-exit-code 1 -P "$recipe/render.py" -- "$original" "$out/build/nail.glb" "$out/renders"
```
