# Major salivary glands and jaw localization

Use the isolated selection to distinguish the three gland pairs and trace the visible gross course of the parotid ducts. Add same-source context to relate the glands to the mandible, masseter and oral floor. Both selections and the [parotid outer envelopes](../../../parotid_glands/exports/source-shared-r01/README.md) share one evaluated source and retain native scale, placement and laterality.

The modeled left submandibular course crosses mylohyoid and the right course remains unaccepted. Both are excluded. The lesson covers gross location and visible course. Duct outlets, lumens, sublingual ducts, tooth reconstruction and clinical use are outside its scope. Colors are illustrative.
