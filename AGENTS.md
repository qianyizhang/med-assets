# Working in med-assets

- **Goal** · Deliver a usable teaching asset or repository change with evidence for its stated scope.
- **Context** · Read [Architecture and workflow](docs/WORKFLOW.md) and the owning asset's README, recipe and review. Use code and manifests for executable behavior. Correct stale docs and report contract violations.
- **Preserve** · Canonical inputs and exports, import identities, coordinates, licenses, public URLs, active PRs and unrelated edits.
- **Completion** · Meet the [applicable gates](docs/WORKFLOW.md#verification), verify changed failure paths and deliver a scoped commit and draft PR with the tested revision, results and limits.
- **Decisions** · Continue on reversible assumptions within scope and report material ones. Resolve scope changes, destructive actions, guarded execution, uploads, publication and merge under their applicable authorization.
